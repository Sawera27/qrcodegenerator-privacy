Privacy Policy

App: QR Code Maker
Developer:Sa'Al
Effective date:November 17, 2026

QR Code Maker ("the App") creates QR codes from links or phone numbers that you type in. Your privacy matters. This policy explains what the App does and does not do with information.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal information. The App has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no internet access. We do not have servers that receive your data.

How the App works

The text you enter (a link or phone number) is turned into a QR code entirely on your device. It is never sent anywhere and is not kept after you close the App.

Permissions

The App does not request any Android permissions. It does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, location or files.

Saving and sharing

  • Save: when you tap Save, the QR code image is stored in your device's gallery (Pictures/QR Code Maker). It stays on your device and under your control.
  • Share: when you tap Share, the QR code image is handed to the app you choose (for example WhatsApp). That app's own privacy policy applies to what it does with the image. We do not receive it.

Children's privacy

The App does not collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this page with a new effective date.
